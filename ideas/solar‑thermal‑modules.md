## Thermosiphon Systems
Passive solar water circulation using gravity and density differences.

**Key Principles**
- Collector must sit below the storage tank  
- Pipes must slope continuously upward  
- Works with flat‑plate collectors  
- No pumps, wiring, or controls  

**Why it matters**  
Ideal for Phase 1–2. Extremely reliable, freeze‑resistant when designed correctly.

Learn more: [Explore thermosiphon systems](ca://s?q=Explore_thermosiphon_systems)

## Tracking Systems
Concentrators require alignment; tracking is the most failure‑prone subsystem.

**Failure Modes**
- Wandering focal points  
- Receiver overheating  
- Fire hazards  
- Mechanical wear  

**Resilient Approaches**
- CPC (Compound Parabolic Concentrators)  
- Passive bimetallic tracking  
- Gravity‑driven equatorial mounts  
- Manual counterweight tracking
