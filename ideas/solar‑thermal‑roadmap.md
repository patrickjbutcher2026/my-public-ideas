# Village‑Scale Solar Thermal Roadmap
A high‑level progression from low‑tech heat to industrial‑level mechanical power.

## Phase 1 — Sanitation & Survival
Flat‑plate collectors, thermosiphon, hot water, hygiene.

## Phase 2 — Food & Climate
Solar ovens, dehydration, IcyBall refrigeration.

## Phase 2.5 — Bootstrap Engines
Atmospheric engines, oscillating engines, crude hot‑air engines, solar piston engines.

## Phase 3 — Mechanical Dawn
Parabolic troughs, Fresnel reflectors, small steam engines.

## Phase 4 — Industrial Micro‑Grid
Solar tower, heliostats, molten salt storage, 24/7 steam power.

## Thermal Storage Overview
Hot water → rock beds → thermal oil → molten salts → mechanical storage.

## Industrial Ladder
Heat → Food → Cooling → Crude Engines → Precision Engines → Electricity → Industry
