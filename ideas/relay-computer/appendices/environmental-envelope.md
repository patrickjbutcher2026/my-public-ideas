# Environmental Envelope

This appendix defines the environmental conditions under which the relay computer is expected to operate reliably. These limits ensure the machine remains functional in workshops, civic offices, community centers, and other non‑laboratory environments.

The environmental envelope is intentionally broad to support long‑term, real‑world use.

---

## Temperature Range

### Operational  
0–40°C  
The machine performs reliably within this range without special cooling or heating.

### Storage  
–10–50°C  
Components tolerate wider temperatures when not in operation.

### Notes  
- Below 0°C: lubrication thickens, relay timing slows  
- Above 40°C: coil heating increases, contact wear accelerates  

---

## Humidity Range

### Operational  
10–80% relative humidity  
Condensation must be avoided.

### Storage  
5–90% relative humidity  
Tape should be stored in sealed containers in high‑humidity regions.

### Notes  
- High humidity increases oxidation on relay contacts  
- Low humidity increases static discharge risk (minor but noted)

---

## Dust and Particulates

The machine tolerates moderate dust loads.

### Acceptable Conditions  
- woodworking shops  
- civic offices  
- community workshops  
- classrooms  

### Unacceptable Conditions  
- metal grinding areas  
- environments with conductive dust  
- heavy soot or ash exposure  

Dust accumulation must be logged and cleaned during weekly maintenance.

---

## Vibration and Shock

### Operational  
Low to moderate vibration is acceptable.

### Limits  
- Continuous vibration: must not cause relay chatter  
- Shock events: machine must be re‑inspected after any drop or impact  

The frame includes vibration‑damped feet to reduce transmission.

---

## Light Exposure

### Acceptable  
- indoor lighting  
- indirect sunlight  

### Avoid  
- prolonged direct sunlight on tape  
- UV exposure that may embrittle plastics or paper  

Indicators must remain visible under normal room lighting.

---

## Airflow and Ventilation

The machine requires passive airflow.

### Requirements  
- 10–20 cm clearance on all sides  
- no enclosed cabinets without ventilation  
- avoid placing near heaters or vents  

Airflow prevents heat buildup in relay banks.

---

## Power Environment

### Acceptable Sources  
- hand‑crank generator  
- pedal generator  
- 12 V DC supply  
- solar‑buffered microgrid  

### Electrical Noise  
Ripple must remain below 0.5 V peak‑to‑peak to prevent relay chatter.

---

## Environmental Monitoring

Operators should periodically record:

- temperature  
- humidity  
- dust load  
- power stability  

These values are logged in the mechanics logbook.

---

## Summary

The environmental envelope ensures the relay computer remains:

- reliable  
- predictable  
- maintainable  
- suitable for civic and community environments  

These conditions support long‑term operation without specialized facilities.
