# Dimensional Guidelines

This appendix defines the recommended physical dimensions, clearances, spacing, and layout conventions for constructing the relay computer. These guidelines ensure the machine remains maintainable, inspectable, and buildable using workshop‑level tools.

Dimensions are intentionally approximate. The machine is designed for tolerance, not precision.

---

## Overall System Footprint

### Recommended Dimensions
- Width: 60–90 cm  
- Height: 40–60 cm  
- Depth: 25–40 cm  

### Notes
- Larger frames improve access and visibility  
- Smaller frames increase wiring density and reduce serviceability  

---

## Relay Bank Dimensions

### Relay Spacing
- Horizontal spacing: 20–30 mm center‑to‑center  
- Vertical spacing: 20–25 mm  
- Clearance above relays: 10–20 mm for indicators  

### Mounting Rails
- Wood or metal rails spaced 80–120 mm apart  
- Allow 5–10 mm clearance behind relays for wiring  

### Bank Size
- 8–16 relays per bank recommended  
- Larger banks become difficult to service  

---

## Register Bank Dimensions

### Register Spacing
- 25–35 mm per register  
- Indicators must remain visible from 1–2 meters away  

### Latch Arrangement
- Input and output latches aligned horizontally  
- Pre‑commit latch placed directly above or below  

### Manual Reset Clearance
- 15–20 mm clearance for finger access  

---

## ALU Dimensions

### Summing Mechanism
- Gear diameter: 20–40 mm  
- Shaft spacing: 15–25 mm  
- Lever throw: 10–20 mm  

### Frame
- 100–200 mm wide  
- 60–120 mm tall  
- 20–40 mm deep  

### Notes
- Larger gears improve visibility  
- Smaller gears reduce inertia but are harder to inspect  

---

## Tape Reader Dimensions

### Tape Width
- 20–25 mm total  
- 12 sensing rows + margins  

### Row Spacing
- 3–4 mm center‑to‑center  

### Hole Diameter
- 1.5–2 mm  

### Sprocket Wheel
- Diameter: 20–30 mm  
- Tooth pitch: matches tape frame spacing  

### Tape Path Clearance
- 0.5–1 mm lateral clearance  
- 1–2 mm vertical clearance  

---

## Plugboard Dimensions

### Board Size
- 150–300 mm wide  
- 100–200 mm tall  

### Socket Grid
- Spacing: 10–15 mm  
- Rows: 8–16  
- Columns: 10–20  

### Patch Cords
- Length: 80–200 mm  
- Diameter: 2–4 mm  

### Notes
- Larger spacing improves clarity  
- Smaller spacing increases routing density  

---

## Wiring and Looms

### Wire Lengths
- Subsystem interconnects: 150–400 mm  
- Internal subsystem wiring: 50–150 mm  

### Loom Bundles
- Diameter: 5–15 mm  
- Secured every 80–150 mm  

### Clearance
- 5–10 mm between looms  
- Avoid tight bends (< 10 mm radius)  

---

## Output Unit Dimensions

### Typewheel or Printer
- Wheel diameter: 40–80 mm  
- Character width: 2–4 mm  
- Paper feed width: 40–60 mm  

### Mechanism Clearance
- 10–20 mm around moving parts  

---

## Frame and Access

### Panel Thickness
- Wood: 6–12 mm  
- Metal: 1–2 mm  

### Access Panels
- Hinged or removable  
- Minimum opening: 120 × 120 mm  

### Feet and Mounting
- Height: 10–20 mm  
- Vibration‑damped if possible  

---

## Human Factors

### Viewing Distance
- Indicators readable at 1–2 meters  

### Reach
- All controls reachable within 40–60 cm  

### Label Size
- Minimum 4–6 mm character height  

---

## Summary

These dimensional guidelines ensure the relay computer remains:

- buildable with hand tools  
- maintainable without disassembly  
- visually inspectable  
- mechanically tolerant  

The dimensions are recommendations, not requirements — the architecture is intentionally flexible.
