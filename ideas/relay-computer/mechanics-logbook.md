# Mechanics Logbook

This logbook defines the structure, expectations, and procedures for documenting all mechanical, electrical, and operational maintenance performed on the relay computer. It is a living record and an essential part of the machine’s long‑term reliability.

The logbook ensures continuity across operators, shifts, and generations.

---

## Purpose of the Logbook

The logbook exists to:

- record all maintenance actions  
- track drift and wear over time  
- provide accountability and traceability  
- support training of new operators  
- preserve institutional memory  

A relay computer is only as trustworthy as its documentation.

---

## Logbook Structure

Each entry in the logbook contains:

- date  
- operator name  
- shift or duty period  
- tasks performed  
- anomalies observed  
- parts replaced  
- calibration results  
- follow‑up actions required  

Entries must be written clearly and legibly.

## Recording Anomalies

All anomalies must be logged, no matter how small. Examples include:

- relay chatter  
- inconsistent tape advance  
- ALU hesitation  
- increased power draw  
- intermittent register updates  
- audible timing irregularities  

Operators must describe:

- what happened  
- when it happened  
- what the machine was doing  
- whether it was repeatable  
- what immediate action was taken  

---

## Parts Replacement Log

Whenever a part is replaced, record:

- part name  
- part source  
- reason for replacement  
- old part condition  
- new part specifications  
- operator performing replacement  

This ensures traceability and helps future operators understand drift patterns.

---

## Calibration Records

Calibration entries must include:

- tape used  
- expected results  
- observed results  
- adjustments made  
- post‑adjustment verification  

Calibration tapes are stored with the logbook.

---

## Operator Conduct

Operators must:

- write clearly  
- avoid shorthand unless standardized  
- never erase entries (strike through instead)  
- sign every entry  
- report anomalies immediately  

The logbook is a legal and civic document.

---

## Summary

The mechanics logbook is the relay computer’s memory of its own physical life. It ensures:

- continuity  
- accountability  
- reliability  
- transparency  

A well‑kept logbook is as important as the machine itself.
