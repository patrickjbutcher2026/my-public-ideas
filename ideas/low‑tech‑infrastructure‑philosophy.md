# Low‑Tech Infrastructure Philosophy

## Core Principles
Legibility, safe failure, embodied constraints, communal stewardship.

## Pillars
- Windhouse (wind power)
- Solarhouse (solar thermal)
- Energy Shed (mechanical routing)
- Relay Computer (information infrastructure)
- Village Industrial Ladder (development sequence)

## Goal
A resilient, post‑electronic, human‑centered industrial ecosystem.
