# Listening Device Specification — Version 2.0  
**Audio Guild — Playback Hardware Division**

Version 2.0 of the Listening Device Specification defines the standardized mechanical and acoustic design for all Guild‑approved playback machines.  
It replaces Version 1.0 by improving stability, intelligibility, and manufacturability while preserving full compatibility with all existing Guild records.

This document covers mechanical structure, acoustic path, stylus assembly, platter system, governor integration, and maintenance requirements.

---

# 1. Purpose

The Listening Device (LD‑2) is designed to:

- provide stable, intelligible playback of survival‑grade shellac records  
- operate without electricity  
- be manufacturable in any settlement with basic metal and wood tools  
- maintain tonal consistency across settlements  
- minimize groove wear  
- integrate with Calibration Day procedures  
- support archival listening without damage  

LD‑2 is the **standard playback machine** for all Guild settlements.

---

# 2. System Overview

The LD‑2 consists of:

1. **Platter Assembly**  
2. **Speed Governor**  
3. **Tonearm & Stylus Assembly**  
4. **Acoustic Horn Path**  
5. **Chassis & Vibration Control**  
6. **User Controls**  

Each subsystem is defined in detail below.

---

# 3. Platter Assembly

## 3.1 Platter Material  
Approved materials:

- cast iron (preferred)  
- bronze  
- hardwood with metal rim (fallback)  

## 3.2 Dimensions  
- Diameter: **10–12 inches**  
- Thickness: **8–12 mm**  
- Mass: **1.5–3 kg** (higher mass improves stability)

## 3.3 Bearing System  
- hardened steel spindle  
- bronze or brass sleeve bearing  
- lubrication channels  
- dust cap or felt seal  

## 3.4 Strobe Markings  
All platters must include:

- radial strobe markings  
- calibrated for settlement’s standard speed  
- visible under flickering lamp or sunlight through slats  

These markings are essential for Calibration Day.

---

# 4. Speed Governor Integration

The LD‑2 must include a governor meeting the **Speed Governor Standard**.

## 4.1 Mounting  
- governor mounted directly to platter shaft  
- vibration‑isolated bracket  
- accessible tension adjustment screw  

## 4.2 Performance  
- stable speed within ±1%  
- minimal flutter  
- smooth response to stylus drag  

## 4.3 User Adjustment  
- external tension knob  
- marked reference positions  
- lockable setting for archival playback  

---

# 5. Tonearm & Stylus Assembly

## 5.1 Tonearm Geometry  
- length: **180–250 mm**  
- pivot offset: **10–20 mm**  
- tracking angle error minimized by curved or angled arm  
- counterweight adjustable  

## 5.2 Materials  
- hardwood (maple, ash)  
- brass  
- iron  
- composite wood‑metal arms allowed  

## 5.3 Stylus Mount  
- rigid metal holder  
- replaceable stylus  
- damping pad (felt or leather)  
- adjustable vertical tracking force  

## 5.4 Stylus Requirements  
Playback stylus must follow the **Stylus Geometry Standard**:

- conical tip radius: **0.10–0.15 mm**  
- tempered steel  
- polished finish  

## 5.5 Tracking Force  
- standard: **20–40 grams**  
- archival playback: **15–25 grams**  
- heavy force prohibited  

---

# 6. Acoustic Horn Path

The LD‑2 uses a fully mechanical acoustic amplification system.

## 6.1 Diaphragm  
- mica, thin hardwood, or metal foil  
- diameter: **40–60 mm**  
- tension adjustable  
- mounted in airtight housing  

## 6.2 Horn Throat  
- diameter: **8–15 mm**  
- smooth transition from diaphragm chamber  
- no sharp edges  

## 6.3 Horn Body  
Approved materials:

- hardwood  
- sheet metal  
- clay or ceramic  
- composite (wood + metal throat)  

## 6.4 Horn Geometry  
- exponential or conical flare  
- length: **300–600 mm**  
- mouth diameter: **150–300 mm**  

## 6.5 Acoustic Performance  
Horn must provide:

- clear midrange  
- intelligible consonants  
- minimal honk or ringing  
- stable response across 200–4,000 Hz  

---

# 7. Chassis & Vibration Control

## 7.1 Base Material  
- hardwood (oak, ash, maple)  
- cast iron  
- layered composite (wood + felt + metal)

## 7.2 Isolation  
- rubber feet (if available)  
- felt pads  
- sand‑filled compartments (optional)  

## 7.3 Structural Requirements  
- rigid frame  
- no flex under platter load  
- horn mount isolated from platter vibration  

---

# 8. User Controls

LD‑2 includes:

- crank handle (for spring or weight drive)  
- governor tension knob  
- tonearm lift lever  
- stylus access door  
- horn mute flap (optional)  

Controls must be:

- durable  
- intuitive  
- repairable  

---

# 9. Maintenance Requirements

## 9.1 Daily  
- dust platter  
- inspect stylus  
- check tonearm freedom  

## 9.2 Weekly  
- lubricate bearings  
- clean horn throat  
- inspect diaphragm tension  

## 9.3 Monthly (Calibration Day)  
- verify speed  
- check tonal balance  
- inspect stylus under magnification  
- test horn resonance  

## 9.4 Annual  
- replace damping pads  
- re‑seat bearings  
- inspect governor spring  

---

# 10. Failure Modes & Remedies

| Failure Mode | Cause | Remedy |
|--------------|-------|--------|
| Pitch wobble | governor drift | recalibrate tension |
| Distortion | stylus wear | replace stylus |
| Muffled sound | diaphragm slack | tighten screws |
| Harsh treble | horn throat debris | clean throat |
| Rumble | dry bearings | lubricate |
| Flutter | belt slip or governor lag | inspect drive system |
