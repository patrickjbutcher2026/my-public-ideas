# Solarhouse Concept
*A communal solar‑thermal building that captures, stores, and routes heat and mechanical power for a village‑scale infrastructure.*

The Solarhouse is the **solar‑thermal counterpart to the Windhouse**.  
Where the Windhouse turns wind into motion, the Solarhouse turns sunlight into **heat, storage, and mechanical work** — all without electronics.

It is a **building‑scale machine**, a cultural institution, and a resilient energy hub.

---

## ☀️ Purpose
The Solarhouse exists to:

- Capture solar heat  
- Store heat in multiple media  
- Convert heat into mechanical power  
- Route mechanical power to village utilities  
- Provide a communal, legible interface for energy  

It is the **solar heart** of a low‑tech industrial ecosystem.

---

## 🧱 Architectural Principles
The Solarhouse is not a single design — it is a **pattern**.  
Any implementation follows these principles:

### 1. South‑Facing Collector Wall
A large glazed or mirrored surface that feeds:

- Flat‑plate collectors  
- Parabolic troughs  
- CPC concentrators  
- Thermal mass walls  

### 2. Thermal Mass Floor
A thick slab or stone bed that:

- Stores daytime heat  
- Releases warmth at night  
- Stabilizes indoor temperature  

### 3. Rock‑Bed Storage Vault
A buried chamber filled with:

- Stone  
- Brick  
- Rubble  

Heated by hot air or hot oil loops.

### 4. Engine Alcove
A protected corner housing:

- A Phase 2.5 bootstrap engine  
- A Phase 3 steam engine  
- A Stirling engine  

This alcove connects directly to the **Energy Shed** or an internal line shaft.

### 5. Line‑Shaft Corridor
A mechanical “hallway” that distributes power to:

- Pumps  
- Mills  
- Workshop tools  
- Thermal pumps  
- Generators  

### 6. Control Wall (Levers + Valves)
A human‑operated interface:

- Belt shifters  
- Clutches  
- Valve manifolds  
- Dampers  
- Tracking controls  

This is the Solarhouse’s “dashboard.”

## 🔥 Functional Zones
The Solarhouse is divided into zones, each with a clear purpose.

### **1. Heat Capture Zone**
- Collectors  
- Mirrors  
- Troughs  
- CPCs  
- Solar ovens  

### **2. Heat Storage Zone**
- Rock beds  
- Hot‑water tanks  
- Thermal oil reservoirs  
- Molten‑salt vaults (advanced)  

### **3. Mechanical Zone**
- Engines  
- Line shaft  
- Flywheel  
- Belt drives  

### **4. Utility Zone**
- Pumps  
- Compressors  
- Generators  
- Electrolyzers (optional)  

### **5. Communal Zone**
- Shared cooking  
- Seasonal gatherings  
- Apprenticeship instruction

## 🧭 Cultural Role
The Solarhouse is not just infrastructure — it is a **ritual space**.

### Seasonal Rituals
- **First Heat Day** — the first time the rock bed reaches operating temperature  
- **Winter Vigil** — tending the thermal store through the dark months  
- **Summer Boil** — annual steam‑engine maintenance and safety checks  

### Apprenticeship
The Solarhouse teaches:

- Boiler safety  
- Valve timing  
- Heat management  
- Tracking alignment  
- Metallurgy basics  

### Stewardship
A rotating group of caretakers maintains:

- Collectors  
- Engines  
- Storage systems  
- Safety valves  

This creates a **shared energy culture**, not a consumer relationship.
