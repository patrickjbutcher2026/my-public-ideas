# Solarhouse Concept
A communal solar‑thermal building that manages heat, storage, and mechanical power.

## Purpose
- Capture heat
- Store heat
- Convert heat to motion
- Route motion to village utilities

## Architecture
- South‑facing collector wall
- Thermal mass floor
- Rock‑bed storage vault
- Engine alcove
- Line‑shaft corridor
- Control wall (levers + valves)

## Cultural Role
- Seasonal “first heat” ceremony
- Apprenticeship in boiler safety
- Communal cooking days
- Winter resilience rituals
