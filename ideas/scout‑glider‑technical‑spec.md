# Scout Glider Technical Specification
*Engineering details for a post‑industrial reconnaissance glider.*

## Airframe Design
### Structure
- high‑aspect‑ratio wings  
- wooden spars and ribs  
- fabric‑covered surfaces  
- reinforced nose and landing skid  
- lightweight tail boom  

## Weight
- empty weight: 45–70 kg  
- payload: 1 pilot + 5–10 kg gear  

## Performance
- launch speed: 25–35 mph  
- glide ratio: 15:1 to 25:1  
- range (no motor): 10–40 km  
- range (with motor): 20–80 km  

## Optional Sustainer Motor
300–800 W electric motor powered by a small battery pack.

## Launch System
### Ground‑Based Flywheel
Flywheel spun by:

- wood‑gas engine  
- windmill  
- waterwheel  
- pedal power  

### Winch Launch
- cable accelerates glider in 2–4 seconds  
- automatic release  
- 50–150 m track  

## Landing Requirements
- grass, dirt, snow, or beach  
- skid or small wheel  
- reinforced belly  

## Maintenance
### Daily
- inspect fabric  
- check control cables  
- inspect wing attachment points  

### Weekly
- lubricate bearings  
- inspect winch cable  

### Seasonal
- re‑dope fabric  
- inspect wooden spars  
- check flywheel housing  

## Range Analysis
### Minimum guaranteed range
2.5–5 miles (dead air)

### Normal scouting radius
10–20 miles

### Best‑case total distance
30–60+ miles
