# Rammed‑Earth Icehouse
*A passive cold‑storage pattern using earth mass, seasonal ice, and controlled airflow.*

## Purpose
A rammed‑earth icehouse provides long‑term cold storage without electricity. Thick earthen walls, partial burial, and seasonal ice retention create a stable low‑temperature environment.

## Core Principles
- **Thermal mass** slows heat transfer  
- **Earth burial** stabilizes temperature  
- **North orientation** minimizes solar gain  
- **Ice trench or ice well** stores winter ice  
- **Controlled airflow** prevents moisture buildup  

## Architectural Pattern
- Thick rammed‑earth walls (high thermal inertia)  
- Semi‑buried structure with insulated roof  
- North‑facing entrance  
- Meltwater drainage channel  
- Chest‑height storage for accessibility  
- Optional pull‑out insulated drawers  

## Seasonal Behavior
- **Winter:** Ice is harvested and stored  
- **Spring:** Icehouse remains cold through thermal inertia  
- **Summer:** Slow melt provides months of refrigeration  
- **Autumn:** Structure cools naturally as temperatures drop  

## Suitable Storage
- dairy  
- beverages  
- cured meats  
- temperature‑sensitive foods  
- seed storage  

## Maintenance
- annual ice harvest  
- drainage inspection  
- interior cleaning  
- monitoring for heat leaks  

## Integration Potential
Works well with:
- root cellars  
- greenhouses  
- earth‑sheltered food complexes
