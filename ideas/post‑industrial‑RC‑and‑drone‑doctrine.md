# Post‑Industrial RC & Drone Doctrine
*Analog radio, mechanical autopilots, and low‑signature drones for a post‑industrial world.*

## Locator Systems
### Passive Resonant Tag
- LC circuit  
- no power  
- detectable with handheld resonant detector  

### Mechanical Beacon
- wind‑up clicker  
- audible for hundreds of meters  

### LED Beacon (Bimetallic Flasher)
- salvaged LED  
- coin cell  
- mechanical flasher  

### Tethered Locator Kite
- deploys on landing  
- visible from long distances  

## Control Systems
### 1‑Bit Analog Radio
- hand‑cranked transmitter  
- relay‑based receiver  
- simple commands (turn, return, trigger)  

### 2‑Channel Analog Radio
- rudder + elevator trim  
- relay actuators  
- no microchips  

### Tethered Control
- mechanical line  
- simple signal wire  
- ideal for observation  

## Mechanical Autopilots
- pendulum stabilizers  
- clockwork timers  
- cam‑driven rudder shifts  
- barometric capsules  

## Drone Types
### Mechanical Courier Drone
- clockwork route  
- rubber‑band or spring propulsion  

### Flywheel‑Launched Glider Drone
- ground‑powered  
- mechanical timer  
- passive locator  

### Wind‑Powered Autogyro Drone
- autorotation  
- ram‑air turbine  

### Tethered Observation Drone
- kite or balloon  
- persistent surveillance  

## Stealth Characteristics
- no radio emissions  
- no heat signature  
- wood + fabric radar profile  
- slow, bird‑like flight  
- unpredictable to algorithms  
- immune to jamming and hacking  

## Range Envelope
- reliable: 0–1 mile  
- usable: 1–3 miles  
- optimized: 3–6 miles
