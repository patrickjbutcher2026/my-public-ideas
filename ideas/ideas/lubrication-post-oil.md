# Lubrication in a Post‑Oil World (Conceptual Module)

## Purpose
Mechanical systems in a low‑petroleum future still require lubrication.  
This module outlines **low‑tech, renewable lubrication sources** suitable for engines, pulleys, bearings, and compressors.

## Renewable Lubricant Sources
- **Animal fats (tallow, lard)**  
  Stable at low speeds and moderate temperatures.

- **Seed oils (sunflower, flax, canola)**  
  Good for bearings, linkages, and general lubrication.

- **Graphite**  
  Excellent dry lubricant for high‑temperature or dusty environments.

- **Clay‑graphite paste**  
  Traditional piston lubricant for low‑speed engines.

## Applications
- Bearings  
- Pulleys  
- Governor linkages  
- Low‑speed pistons  
- Belt dressing  

## Notes
This is a conceptual overview.  
Exact formulations and tolerances are left to future practitioners.
