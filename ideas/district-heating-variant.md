# District Heating Variant (Conceptual Module)

## Purpose
A wood‑powered energy hub can serve multiple nearby homes through **shared thermal distribution**.

## Concept
- One central fire  
- One thermal battery  
- Underground insulated PEX lines  
- Multiple homes receiving hot water for space heating  

## Benefits
- Reduced wood consumption  
- Reduced labor  
- Centralized maintenance  
- Higher efficiency  

## Scale
Ideal for:
- 2–5 homes  
- Small clusters  
- Shared homestead compounds  

## Notes
This is a conceptual pattern, not a pipe‑sizing or engineering guide.
