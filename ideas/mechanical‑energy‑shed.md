# Mechanical Energy Shed
A line‑shaft‑based, lever‑controlled energy routing system.

## Prime Movers
Steam engine, Stirling, windmill shaft, waterwheel.

## Main Line Shaft
The mechanical “bus” that carries power.

## Branch Loads
- Thermal storage pump
- Generator for DC bus
- Air compressor
- Electrolyzer
- Workshop tools

## Control Interface
Clutches, belt shifters, gear selectors, valve manifolds.

## Human‑Centered Control
Levers on the wall:
- Charge batteries
- Charge compressed air
- Pump hot water
- Run electrolyzer
